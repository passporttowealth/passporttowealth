04Privacy, by default

  • Local first. Your ~/Documents/my-finances/ folder is the source of truth. No cloud sync unless you put the folder in iCloud or Dropbox yourself (the installer warns and offers to relocate if it detects this).
  • Sharing is opt-in. No third-party host is contacted unless you explicitly choose to publish. Most clients never sign up for anything.
  • Sensitive files are skipped. Paystubs, tax returns, and identity documents are not fed into the pipeline by default. The assistant won't read them unless you opt in per file.
  • Always your originals. The dashboard is a view. It never edits or deletes your bank exports — keep your originals as the system of record.
