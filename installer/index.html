Welcome to your Finance Clarity setup

A one-time install on your Mac or Windows PC. About 10 minutes if you've installed developer tools before, up to an hour on a fresh computer.

What this does

The installer sets up everything you need to turn a folder of your financial files into a private, password-protected dashboard you can open from any device. After it finishes, you'll have a START-HERE shortcut on your Desktop. That's the only thing you ever need to remember.

  • Your files stay on your laptop.
  • Only the final dashboard is online — and only people with your passcode can see it.
  • The installer asks how you sign in to Claude (Pro, Max, or API key) and walks you through the rest.

⚠ Mac will warn you about this file — that's normal. Here's what to do:

  1. When you double-click Welcome.command, Mac will say "Welcome.command cannot be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software." Click Cancel.
  2. Open Finder, go to your Downloads folder. Right-click on Welcome.command and choose Open.
  3. Mac asks again, this time with an Open button. Click it.

You only do this the first time. After that, the START-HERE shortcut on your Desktop opens normally.

⚠ Windows will warn you about this file — that's normal. Here's what to do:

  1. When you double-click Welcome.bat, Windows SmartScreen will show a blue dialog: "Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting."
  2. Click More info in the SmartScreen dialog.
  3. A Run anyway button appears. Click it.

You only do this the first time. After that, the START-HERE shortcut on your Desktop opens normally.

Important: Make sure both Welcome.bat and Welcome.ps1 are in the same folder (your Downloads folder is fine). The .bat file calls the .ps1 next to it.

If something goes wrong

The installer writes a log file and tells you what to do. If you get stuck, contact your advisor with the log file attached.

  • Mac: ~/Library/Logs/passport-to-wealth-install.log
  • Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\PassportToWealth\Logs\passport-to-wealth-install.log
