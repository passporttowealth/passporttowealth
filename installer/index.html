What this does

The installer sets up everything quietly behind the scenes — Python, the AI assistant, the dashboard skill — so you only ever see a single shortcut on your Desktop. After it finishes, the workflow is one sentence:

  • Drop your bank statements, payslips, or tax docs into one folder.
  • Tell the assistant "build my report."
  • It sorts, dedupes, normalizes currencies, fetches exchange rates, categorizes, and shows the totals back for confirmation.
  • Once you approve, it builds a branded dashboard and opens it in your browser.

No app to learn. No spreadsheet to maintain. Refreshes are "I added new files. Refresh."

How it works

01 — Local

On your laptop

Your files stay on your computer. The pipeline runs locally. Nothing is uploaded by default.

02 — Private

Opens in your browser

The dashboard opens from disk via a file:// URL. No third-party server, no signup, no passcode.

03 — Optional

Share if you want

Want to send it to your advisor or family? One sentence puts it on a private URL behind a passcode.

Privacy

Local by default. The ~/Documents/my-finances/ folder is the source of truth. Nothing syncs to a cloud unless you put the folder inside iCloud or Dropbox yourself (the installer warns and offers to relocate if it detects this).

Sharing is opt-in. No third-party host is contacted unless you explicitly choose to publish your dashboard. Users who never publish never sign up for anything.

Sensitive files are skipped. Paystubs, tax returns, and identity documents are not fed into the pipeline by default — the AI assistant won't read them unless you opt in per file.

Help, this didn't work

If something fails, the installer writes a log file and prints what to do. If you get stuck, contact your advisor with the log file attached:

  • macOS: ~/Library/Logs/passport-to-wealth-install.log
  • Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\PassportToWealth\Logs\passport-to-wealth-install.log

Can't open Terminal or PowerShell? Use the file-download fallback instead (legacy installers in the GitHub repo's installer/legacy/ folder).
